01 / about

Who I am & what I build

I'm a 3rd-year CS undergrad who got into software because I wanted to understand how things actually work — not just consume them.

Most of my time goes into systems with real architecture decisions: API design, database schemas, caching strategies, ML integration. I'm building toward ML/AI research and a European master's.

My standard: if I can't explain why a design decision was made, I haven't understood it well enough to ship it.

API Design & Infrastructure
REST APIs, database schemas, caching strategies, rate limiting — built to be reliable and maintainable, not just impressive in a README.
ML Engineering
Integrating models into production systems. I care about the pipeline, retraining loops, and tradeoffs — not just the accuracy score.
Systems Thinking
Architecture, failure modes, and design decisions. I think about what breaks first, not just what ships first.
